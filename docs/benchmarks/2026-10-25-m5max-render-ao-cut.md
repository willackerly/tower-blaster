# M5 Max render bench: AO cost cut (32f3660)

This ran on the Mac in real Chrome (Metal-3) at 2560x1440. At 5cd751a, AO cost 0.43 ms of the near-100k frame and 0.90 ms of the far-100k frame (GPU frame p50, full minus no-ao). Most of that was per-pixel work in the scene pass:
- the seam cavity's extra tap, taken on most pixels of the far view;
- the grid's voxels below the floor.

At 32f3660, three things changed:
- the seam fades out under 2 px of band;
- the grid stops at the floor;
- the own footprint is derived in the fragment shader.

GPU frame p50 at 32f3660 (5cd751a in brackets):

| World | Bodies | Full (ms) | No-AO (ms) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near | 10k | 1.066 [1.108] | 0.824 [0.797] |
| Near | 100k | 2.157 [2.165] | 1.753 [1.730] |
| Far | 10k | 0.671 [0.759] | 0.526 [0.517] |
| Far | 100k | 2.313 [2.664] | 1.818 [1.765] |

AO's share of the frame:

| World | Bodies | 32f3660 | 5cd751a |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near | 10k | 22.5% | 27.6% |
| Near | 100k | 15.5% | 19.6% |
| Far | 10k | 20.9% | 31.9% |
| Far | 100k | 21.8% | 33.4% |

AO cost in the cull pass at 100k, full minus no-ao:
- near: 0.092 ms, down from 0.110;
- far: 0.119 ms, down from 0.200 (the clipped grid).

Two variants measure the parts that remain:
- **no-seam:** the seam still costs 5-8% at close range, where its band is several pixels wide. That is a visual feature, so it is kept.
- **own-read:** reading the own centre and footprint per pixel from the per-slot buffers, as before, is not significantly different at near range. It is 2.1-2.3% *faster* in the far view than deriving them from the fragment's inputs: the derivation is exact (tested) but costs aoFoot's ALU on every pixel. So the read path stays, and the derived path was removed in the next commit.
