# M5 Max render bench with volume AO (cfdbcf6)

Run by the Mac session on 2026-10-25 in real Chrome (Metal-3) at 2560x1440. GPU frame p50, full vs no-ao: near 10k 1.004 vs 0.791 ms, near 100k 2.079 vs 1.814, far 10k 0.761 vs 0.529, far 100k 2.366 vs 1.904. The cost sits in the cull pass, which went from 0.115 to 0.347 ms at near 100k and from 0.117 to 0.518 at far 100k. It is mostly compose: one thread per column read up to 64 voxels per step in series at the 2 m level. With AO off the scene pass was still 0.21 ms over 91f28ff. That is the two flat vertex outputs added for own subtraction: a tiler stores every vertex output, and the cube path, with 36 vertices per box, lost its lead over the fan. Both were fixed in the next commit.
