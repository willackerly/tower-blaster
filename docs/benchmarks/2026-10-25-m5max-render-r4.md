# M5 Max render bench with R4 on (bf1ff1a)

Run by the Mac session on 2026-10-25 in real Chrome (Metal-3) at 2560x1440. The frame grew 17-40% over bb1add0. The scene pass at near 100k went from 0.89 to 1.15 ms and the cull pass from 0.077 to 0.114 ms. Two likely causes were fixed in the next commit:

- every scene, box and ground fragment looped over all 12 light slots from a storage buffer, even with none lit;
- the streak draw drew all 64k pool instances every frame (about 0.035 ms of the scene pass, the full minus no-sparks gap at near 100k).

The cull growth is the spark detector (no-sparks cull 0.082 ms). The raw report follows.
