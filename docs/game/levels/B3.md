# B3 Ten Thousand

The third big level of the spectacle slice (roadmap G5.4, [spectacle-mode.md](../spectacle-mode.md) section 11), and the slice's one true 10k level. Play it at `?level=B3` (game), or play on from B2.

## Settings

| | |
|---|---|
| Structure | 10,000 stone cubes: a city of towers 5 x 5 x 25 with streets 3 (draft B3d) |
| Rack | three bombs (`BOMB`) |
| Objective (★) | collapse at least 0.4 |
| Stars metric | collapse |
| Physics | the game's defaults: GPU solver, 40 iterations, settle v3 |
| Level hash | `f11e947a868b602f` |

How it was chosen (Metal explorations B3a to B3e, spectacle-mode.md section 11): the third item (a wrecking ball or a ram) added nothing after two bombs; the skyline stayed 1.0 in every play (some tower always stands), so B3 is judged on collapse; 20 iterations bought nothing over 40. A play is about 1 min on the M5 (20 ms a step), so a short-recipe cast is about 3 to 3.5 h against the 60 min budget gate, raised for this level (`--budget-min`).

## What the two casts say

- **The climbs found different lines.** Seed 2's line is good in its median (0.69) but has a long lower tail (P10 0.40), so its T3 is low. Seed 1's line is the better one by T3. The recast gate assumes two seeds find the same basin; on B3 (and B2) they do not, so it fails for a reason that says nothing about the stars' noise.
- **Skill pays little on B3.** Pooling every competent play (128) gives T2 0.535; seed 1's line gives T3 0.634; the gap is 0.099 against the gate's 0.100. Three bombs into a 10k stone city do roughly the same damage wherever they land: aiming the best line known brings down about 10% more of the city than a typical attempt.
- **Stable is sampling noise of seed 1's cloud**, which more nudges of the same line fix (`--extend`; the interval narrows as 1/sqrt(n): 100 nudges, about 0.037 expected).
