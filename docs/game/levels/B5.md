# B5 Kapla Spire (in design)

The fifth big level of the spectacle slice (spectacle-mode.md section 11, roadmap B5): S15's Kapla tower at about 3,000 planks, judged on collapse. **Not on the level door until its stars are cast** (PT7: no empty star rows); its drafts play as unrated trials (`?level=T-B5A`).

## Scale: why the planks are 13 times real Kapla

S15 is built in plank widths (a real Kapla plank is 117 x 23.4 x 7.8 mm). At that size the GPU solver needs about 7 substeps a frame and E2b's sleep stabiliser to hold a tower (E5, E2b); the game steps once a frame. So B5 places S15 at h = 0.3 m a plank width: a plank is 1.5 x 0.3 x 0.1 m, the cross-section of X3's Kapla cribs (T-X3K), which the game already plays at one substep. A body 13 times bigger falls sqrt(13) = 3.6 times slower, so B5's collapse is a toy tower's in **declared slow motion** (the verify line's alternative to real time). GPU sleep is on (`physics.sleep`), as E2b's gate requires.

Alternatives considered:
- **Real Kapla size with substeps.** About 7 times the step cost at 3,000 planks, and the game's loop steps once a frame; rejected for the slice.
- **h 0.2 m** (planks 6.7 cm thick): a shorter tower (26 m), but thinner planks than anything the game plays at one substep; kept as a fallback if the 0.3 m tower is too tall to frame.
- **A new wood material.** Kapla is pine (about 450 kg/m3, friction about 0.45); materials are shared with rendering and sound, so the drafts use plaster (250, 0.6) and a wood material waits on the exploration showing it matters.

## Shape and launcher

| Draft | Shape | Planks | Height | Across | Sleep |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| B5a | one spire, slenderness 3 | 2,884 | 37.5 m | 15 m | on |
| B5b | five towers, slenderness 3 | 3,106 | 39.3 m | 21 m | on |
| B5c | B5a | 2,884 | 37.5 m | 15 m | **off** (does it stand at all?) |

At 3,000 planks S15's spire is 26 to 65 m tall depending on slenderness; 3 keeps it under 40 m. A marble (15 m/s) thrown from the ground reaches only the lower third, so the competent player's shots reached their target 28 to 47% of the time from the default launcher (0.6 m up). The launcher stands 90 h (27 m) up: 81% (B5a) and 88% (B5b), the best of 15 to 33 m for this rack (CI: `drafts.test.ts` needs 80%). The game's default launch mode (high) starts above the top anyway.

## Rack

Marbles and a grenade (`marble`, `marble`, `marble`, `w-grenade`, as T-X3K: planks are light). The spec's toy-scale balls do not fit at this scale: the beach ball is a dud (one head-on hit at full power is 7 J and cannot tip a plank: `validateLevel`), and competent bowling-ball and basketball shots reach their target 56% of the time at best.

## Proofs

- **CI:** `keystone-lab/test/drafts.test.ts`: the drafts compile and validate clean on the GPU budget, plank size, unit, launcher and sleep as above, and at least 80% of competent shots reach their target. They are trials T-B5A to T-B5C (`trial-levels.test.ts`).
- **Rest (Metal, or SwiftShader with `B5_REST=1`):** `keystone-lab/test/b5-gpu.test.ts`: B5a and B5b stand after their pre-settle (no plank moves 0.05 h, the top holds, no limit hit); B5c is reported. SwiftShader, 300 pre-settle + 100 steps (2026-11-07): B5a and B5b move 0 (asleep after the pre-settle), no limit; B5c (no sleep) moves at most 0.024 h (7 mm) and its top rises 0.024 h, no limit. 100 steps is 1.7 s of game time, so this says the spires load standing, not that they hold for a minute: the Metal run (600 steps) and E2b's idle-hold gate are the proof of that.
